Cat Walk puts a single cat on your screen. It wanders around while you work, so you can glance at a small, moving friend without stopping what you are doing.

MAC
- Lives in the menu bar and stays out of the Dock
- The cat walks, stops, sits, sleeps, and grooms along the bottom of your screen
- Clicks anywhere except on the cat go straight through to the windows behind it, so it never gets in your way
- Shows up on every desktop, over other apps in full screen, and on multiple displays
- Drag the cat to pick it up and set it down anywhere
- In Settings, choose whether to show the cat, its size (small, medium, large), its walking speed (leisurely, normal, fast), and whether to launch at login

IPHONE
- Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets where the cat changes its pose and spot over time
- Take it for a walk and the cat strolls across a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
- Tap the cat in the app to pet it and make its day

APPLE WATCH
- The same cat appears in a complication and in the watch app

PEACE OF MIND
- No sign-in and no account
- No ads
- No data collected and nothing sent anywhere (your settings stay on your device)
