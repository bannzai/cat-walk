# Privacy Policy

bannzai (the "Provider") establishes this Privacy Policy (this "Policy") regarding the handling of user information, including personal information, of users of the service "Cat Walk" (the "Service") provided by the Provider.

## User Information Collected and Collection Methods
In this Policy, "user information" means information relating to the identification of users, behavioral history on communication services, and other information generated or accumulated in relation to users or their devices, which the Provider collects in accordance with this Policy.

### Information Entered by Users (Stored Only on Your Device)
The settings you configure in the Service, such as the cat's visibility, size, walking speed, and position, are **stored only on your device (in `UserDefaults`) and are not transmitted to the Provider's servers**. The Provider cannot collect or view this information.

- Retention period: stored on your device until you delete the app
- Deletion procedure: deleting the app from your device also deletes these settings

### External Services
The Service does not use any external analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not perform network communication.

### Information Collected by the Provider
The Service does not require account registration, and the Provider does not collect or store users' personal information on servers. Only when you contact the Provider by email or other means, the Provider uses your contact information and the content of your inquiry to respond to it.

## Purposes of Use
The specific purposes of use of user information are as follows:

- To provide information about the Service and respond to inquiries
- To respond to acts that violate the Provider's terms, policies, or other rules regarding the Service
- To notify users of changes to the terms and other rules regarding the Service

## Provision to Third Parties
The Provider will not provide personal information among user information to third parties (including those located outside Japan) without obtaining the user's prior consent. However, this does not apply in the following cases where provision to third parties (including those located outside Japan) is necessary:

- Where personal information is provided in connection with the succession of business due to a merger or other reasons
- Where it is necessary to cooperate with a national government agency, a local government, or a party entrusted by them in executing affairs prescribed by laws and regulations, and obtaining the user's consent is likely to impede the execution of such affairs
- Other cases permitted by the Act on the Protection of Personal Information of Japan (the "Personal Information Protection Act") or other laws and regulations

## Disclosure, Correction, Suspension of Use, and Deletion of Personal Information
When the Provider is requested by a user to disclose, correct, suspend the use of, or delete personal information based on the provisions of the Personal Information Protection Act, the Provider will respond without delay after confirming that the request is made by the user themselves (if such personal information does not exist, the Provider will notify the user to that effect). However, this does not apply where the Provider is not obligated to do so under the Personal Information Protection Act or other laws and regulations.

Settings stored only on your device are not held by the Provider, so you can delete them yourself by deleting the app.

The Provider will change this Policy as necessary. When changing this Policy, the Provider will announce the effective date and content of the changed Policy by displaying it in the app or by other appropriate means, or will notify users.
